Challenger Astronauts Memorialized on the Moon
Apollo is a 524 km-wide impact basin located inside the giant South Pole–Aitken basin on the Moon. NASA identified it as a potential region for future human lunar exploration. After the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster, seven craters on Apollo Basin's eastern rim were named in honor of the seven crew members who lost their lives.

Apollo 8’s Iconic Earthrise
The Apollo 8 crew captured a rising Earth about five degrees above the Moon’s horizon near 110° east longitude. Taken on December 24 at 15:40 GMT, the image shows Earth's sunset line crossing Africa, the South Pole near its left end, and clouds covering North and South America.
